### 🚀 About Me
I have a **MSc in Clinical Psychology** and I am building a research-focused
portfolio at the intersection of **cognitive neuroscience**, **behavioural science**,
**digital health** and **human-centred technology**.

💡 I enjoy behavioural research using eye-tracking, and continuous learning.   
🧠 I am currently strengthening my skills in Python, EEG analysis, and experimental programming.   
🎯 Looking for **entry-level research roles**, **collaborations**, and **funded graduate opportunities** .   
🥗 Outside research and coding, I enjoy cooking and testing new recipes, traveling, and exploring art and design.
